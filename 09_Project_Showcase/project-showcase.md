# Project Showcase: AI SEO Blog & Content Cluster Generator

## Project Summary
For Future Interns Prompt Engineering Task 3, this repository demonstrates a reusable prompt workflow for producing SEO content for a local business website.

## Problem
Local business content can become scattered: articles may target overlapping keywords, miss the user's search intent, or make unsupported claims about services and facilities.

## Approach
The workflow organizes content into a business brief, keyword hypotheses, search intent map, cluster plan, reusable prompts, one pillar blog, four supporting blogs, internal-link suggestions, and a local SEO QA checklist.

## Deliverables
- Business brief and audience assumptions
- Keyword map and intent classification
- Pillar-and-supporting-article cluster
- Reusable prompt templates
- One pillar blog draft
- Four supporting blog drafts
- Internal linking plan
- Local SEO checklist and final content pack

## Skills Demonstrated
- Prompt engineering
- SEO content planning
- Search intent mapping
- H1–H3 blog structuring
- Content cluster planning
- Internal linking
- Local SEO quality checks
- Documentation in GitHub

## Limitations and Next Steps
The keyword phrases are hypotheses and have not been validated with measured search volume or difficulty. The business's current details have not been independently verified in this sample. Next steps are to validate facts and keywords, replace placeholders with official URLs, edit the drafts with the business owner, publish, and measure performance.
