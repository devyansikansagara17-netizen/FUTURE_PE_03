## Primary Topic
A helpful guide to choosing a cafe in Ahmedabad.

## Primary Keyword Hypothesis
“cafe in Chandkheda Ahmedabad” — validate with current search results and keyword tools before final use.

## Content Cluster Summary
- Pillar: A Helpful Guide to Choosing a Cafe in Ahmedabad
- Supporting 1: How to Choose a Cafe for Studying or Working
- Supporting 2: Planning a Casual Cafe Meetup With Friends
- Supporting 3: How to Explore Coffee, Snacks, and Desserts at a Cafe
- Supporting 4: What to Check Before Visiting a Local Cafe

## Prompt Workflow
Business brief → keyword hypotheses → search intent → content cluster → H1–H3 outline → long-form draft → internal links → fact-checking → final pack.

## Publication Requirements
Before any public/business use:
- Verify the current business name, address, services, menu, hours, and facilities.
- Validate keyword relevance and record data sources/date if metrics are used.
- Replace all CTA placeholders with real official URLs.
- Add only factual claims supported by reliable sources.
- Proofread all content and check for originality.
- Ensure each article answers a distinct reader question.
- Revisit content when business information changes.

## Limitations
This pack is a structured first draft, not a guarantee of search ranking. Keyword metrics, competitor analysis, and performance data have not been independently measured in this sample.
