|---|---|---|---|
| cafe in Chandkheda Ahmedabad | Local / visit-oriented | Nearby options and reliable visit details | Accurate location/contact details and a helpful local guide |

| things to check before visiting a cafe | Informational | Avoiding surprises | Checklist: hours, address, menu, payment, seating, and contact information |

## Intent Rules
- Answer the query near the beginning of the page.
- Do not force a sales pitch into a purely informational article.
- Use the business name only where it naturally helps.
- Add calls to action only when there is a real, verified destination page.
- Do not imply the business offers facilities or menu items until confirmed.
