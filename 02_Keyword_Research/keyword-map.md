## Prioritization Method
Use a simple qualitative score from 1–3 after validation:
- **Relevance:** How closely the phrase matches the business and audience.
- **Intent fit:** Whether the page can genuinely satisfy the query.
- **Evidence:** Whether current search results suggest the topic is being searched.

Do not label a keyword “high volume” or “low competition” without data.

## Research Steps to Complete
1. Search each phrase in Google with the target city/area.
2. Review the wording and page types appearing in results.
3. Use Google Trends or a reputable keyword tool if useful.
4. Record date, source, location setting, volume (if provided), and difficulty (if provided).
5. Keep keywords only when they fit the actual business and content.
6. Re-check before publishing because search results and demand change.
