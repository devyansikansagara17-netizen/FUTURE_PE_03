# Business Brief: Beans & Beyond Cafe

## 1. Project Context
- **Business used for this demonstration:** Beans & Beyond Cafe
- **Area:** Chandkheda, Ahmedabad, Gujarat
- **Business category:** Cafe
- **Source of starting context:** A public GitHub Task 3 example identifies this business and area. This repository does not independently verify current business details.
- **Verification status:** Confirm the business name, address, menu, services, opening hours, accessibility, seating, and official website/social profiles before using this content commercially.

## 2. Objective
Build an SEO content system that helps a local cafe answer useful questions from people looking for cafes, coffee, desserts, casual meetups, or a place to work/study in the Chandkheda/Ahmedabad area.

## 3. Intended Audience
1. Local residents searching for a nearby cafe.
2. Students or remote workers looking for a suitable cafe environment.
3. Friends planning a casual meetup.
4. People exploring coffee, beverages, snacks, or desserts.
5. Visitors comparing local cafe options.

These are audience hypotheses for content planning, not verified customer segments.

## 4. Content Goals
- Answer practical questions before a visit.
- Explain how to choose a cafe based on purpose.
- Support discovery through local, natural language.
- Connect related articles through relevant internal links.
- Encourage readers to check current official business information before visiting.

## 5. Brand Voice
- Friendly and welcoming
- Clear, useful, and locally aware
- Not exaggerated or sales-heavy
- Honest about details that need confirmation
- Avoid unsupported superlatives such as “best in Ahmedabad”

## 6. Core Content Pillar
**Working title:** A Helpful Guide to Choosing a Cafe in Ahmedabad

## 7. Content Boundaries
Do not invent or publish unverified:
- Menu items, ingredients, prices, discounts, or offers
- Opening hours, phone numbers, exact address, or booking policy
- Wi-Fi, power outlets, parking, accessibility, pet policy, or seating capacity
- Customer reviews, awards, ratings, or claims about being the “best”
- Health or nutrition claims

## 8. Success Measures
Once content is published and analytics are available, review:
- Search impressions and clicks
- Organic sessions to each article
- Queries bringing users to the site
- Clicks from articles to menu/contact/location pages
- Engagement and conversions that the business can reliably measure

No baseline or target numbers are assumed in this sample.
