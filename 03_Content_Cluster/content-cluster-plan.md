## Cluster Logic
- The pillar provides broad decision-making guidance.
- Each supporting article answers one narrower question in greater detail.
- Supporting articles link back to the pillar using natural descriptive anchor text.
- The pillar links out to relevant supporting articles.
- Menu, contact, location, booking, and policy links must point to real pages only.

## Suggested Publication Sequence
1. Publish the pillar after verifying local facts.
2. Publish S4 to cover practical visit preparation.
3. Publish S1 and S2 based on business suitability and audience relevance.
4. Publish S3 after the current menu has been checked.
5. Review Search Console queries and update the cluster as evidence accumulates.
